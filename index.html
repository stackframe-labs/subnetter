/Subnetter

IPv4 & IPv6 subnet calculator, VLSM planner, and CIDR/mask converter — all in your browser, no data leaves your machine.

IPv4 Subnet Calculator

Enter an IPv4 address and either a CIDR prefix (e.g. 24) or a dotted subnet mask (e.g. 255.255.255.0).

VLSM Planner

Enter a base network in CIDR notation and a comma-separated list of required host counts per subnet. Subnets are allocated largest-first for a correct, non-overlapping fit.

IPv6 Subnet Calculator
